One-pager · for leadership

Approve a phased build of studio-isolated access with a studio-visible audit trail

Start with an immediate fix to shared reporting, then isolate, then prove it to studios.

Problem

Studio content and data are not isolated. Two studios saw competitor names in a shared report. That breaks trust on a top-3 selection factor and blocks a new top studio that requires an access audit to sign.

Goal

Each studio sees only its own games, data and math models, and can verify that through an audit trail.

In scope (MVP)

  • Tenant isolation: every asset owned by one studio; default deny
  • Roles inside each studio (Admin, Viewer, Math/Cert)
  • Studio-scoped reports and exports
  • Access audit log, viewable and exportable by the studio

Out of scope (MVP)

  • Cross-studio benchmarking
  • Third-party certification of controls
  • Studio-managed SSO
